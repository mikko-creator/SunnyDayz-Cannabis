## Round 4 — the round-3 fixes, attacked

15 round-3 claims: 11 held. Four did not and were fixed — store-pill addresses cut with an ellipsis
(R3-pills), blog card images capped at 800w (R3-blog), the single-image ring outlining an empty band
(R3-ring), the home About back frame upscaled 1.36× (R3-frames).

33 defects confirmed; all fixed except five accepted on purpose:
- **D4** hero CTAs below the fold on short desktop windows (1440×657) — reachable after 8–72px of scroll at full opacity; shrinking the H1 there is a design change.
- **D14** the header store pill shows about half the address at 1361–1920 — the full address is one tap away in the chooser.
- **D22, D25, D32** product/city photos whose only source file is 188–408px — no larger file exists.

The majors: content that loaded blurred and stayed blurred until you scrolled (PDP buy box and photo on
all 146 product pages, blog post bodies), the hero pause button covering "Get to know us" at 328–374px,
and no visible way to the store chooser at 1025–1180px.

## Verification rounds 5 and 6 — the fixes' own regressions

The verification pass found regressions in this work, fixed and re-measured:
- one-row breadcrumbs pushed titles and photos past the right edge on 5 page types — **withdrawn**;
- reveals on phones finished 96px late: `view()`'s default inset took the new `html` scroll-padding —
  every `view()` is now `view(block auto 0px)` (0 of 24 → 24 of 24 finish at 30vh);
- the hero button pressed before the page finished loading started the film; rotating a phone kept the
  portrait film upscaled 3.5×; a visitor's own play was lost after scrolling away — all fixed;
- eager banner images delayed first paint on /shop (back to lazy: FCP 2,652 → 2,340 ms vs the regressed build);
- the ZIP left alone on the last line of the store address; the menu button crowding the header at 1025px
  with a classic scrollbar; `sizes` a few vw too high; a blank gap under the zip search; city cards upscaled
  in the new 3-column row; the small-photo PDP stage reading as a box — all fixed;
- legal-page section titles (COMMUNICATIONS, INDEMNIFICATION) clipped at the right edge on phones — this one
  predates the redesign work; fixed (worst right edge 371px → 302px at 320 wide).

The final build is byte-for-byte reproducible (two builds, 924/924 files identical). All 250 page bodies in
`dist/` are identical to the verified build; the SEO pass adds only `twitter:*` meta to each head. Four visual
reviewers each viewed every contact sheet they made (100/100, 36/36, 68/68, 61/61) for 22 page types at
320, 390, 430, 844×390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920, and their skeptics viewed theirs at 360, 414, 600, 1280
and 1366×657.

## Logo size (requested after the first publish)

The header logo grows wherever the nav has room for it, measured per width with a classic 17px scrollbar's
worth kept spare: phones 104 → 128–140px (112px on phones under 600px tall, so the hero buttons stay clear of
the dock), tablets → 152px, 1025–1180 → 128–150px, 1261–1360 → 164px, 1361 and up 136 → 176px. It stays as it
was at 1181–1260, where the store pill leaves under 30px free. The header store pill now shows its badge and
name only; its address line showed about half the address and took the room the logo needed (the full address
is in the store chooser). The footer logo goes 180 → 240px, never wider than its column.

The hero's tuck under the nav is now computed from the logo width instead of three hand-tuned numbers, so the
two cannot drift apart. Checked on 1,666 viewports (320–2560, 1px steps through 1000–1480, two heights) and
481 more with a 17px scrollbar: no overlap, nothing outside the nav, no gap under the announce bar (the old
build had up to 12px), hero copy at least 27px below the nav, no sideways scroll. The detector was proven
able to fire by injecting a 420px logo.

## For the client to decide

- **Age-gate EXIT** goes back when the visitor came from another site, otherwise to `https://www.google.com/`.
  Set `ageGateExitUrl` in `src/content/chrome.json` to change it.
- **Terms and Privacy pages are readable without passing the age gate** (the gate links to them; before,
  they could not be read before agreeing). A compliance call.
- **Design changes on phones** that want a sign-off: a darker veil under the hero copy (text contrast),

  available" and "SEE ALL (0)", and /collection/pill, which is a "Page not found" page on the live site too.

## Known, not fixed

- Landscape phones get the 1080p film (27.8 MB) after load; a 720p landscape rendition would cut it.
- The hero poster is not the film's first frame, so there is a visible cut when playback starts.
- Very short portrait windows (under ~545px tall, e.g. 320×504): the dock still covers the hero's second CTA.
- The PDP strength bars never animate on either build (an ancestor that clips overflow likely becomes their
  scroll timeline; unverified).
- An intermittent layout shift on /collection/flower from a web-font swap (0.07–0.12, rare).
- Not testable here: iOS Safari (`<picture>` media, preload media, focus zoom, the notch), real devices,
  interaction latency. Everything above is headless Chrome with device emulation.
