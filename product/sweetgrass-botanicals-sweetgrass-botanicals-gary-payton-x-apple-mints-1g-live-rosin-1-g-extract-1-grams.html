Product descriptions

Sweetgrass Botanicals delivers premium quality with their Gary Payton x Apple Mints Live Rosin, a 1g concentrate that showcases the art of solventless extraction. This extract combines the legendary genetics of Gary Payton with the refreshing Apple Mints, creating a complex flavor profile that blends sweet, fruity apple notes with cool minty undertones and subtle earthy accents. Live rosin is crafted using ice water hash from fresh frozen cannabis, preserving the plant's full spectrum of cannabinoids and cannabis-derived terpenes for an authentic, potent experience. Expect a smooth, flavorful dab that delivers uplifting euphoria paired with focused relaxation. Perfect for concentrate enthusiasts seeking strain-specific terpenes and exceptional purity, this 1g of Gary Payton x Apple Mints offers connoisseur-grade quality in every session. Sweetgrass Botanicals brings craft cannabis to your collection.
